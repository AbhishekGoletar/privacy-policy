TODOPOMOGORO is designed as a productivity tool combining task management and Pomodoro timing. We prioritize your privacy and minimize data collection:

Your tasks, project names, Pomodoro logs, session histories, and preferences are stored locally on your device storage unless you choose to sync with cloud services. We do not sell or monetize your task data.

Automatically Collected Information

To monitor app health and provide a seamless experience, basic device telemetry and diagnostic information may be gathered automatically when interacting with the Application:

To perform its core functions as a productivity timer, TODOPOMOGORO may request the following permissions:

  • Notifications / Post Notifications: To deliver Pomodoro session completion alerts, break reminders, and task alerts.

3. Third-Party Services

The Application may integrate trusted third-party SDKs to facilitate core functionalities, diagnostics, or analytics. These service providers handle data according to their respective privacy policies:

Because your primary task lists and timer entries are maintained locally on your device, you have complete control over your data:

  • Uninstalling or clearing app storage from your device settings permanently removes all locally stored application data.
